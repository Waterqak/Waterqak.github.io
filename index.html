About me

I build the parts that make the game work.

“Scripter” is the short title. In practice, I work across the systems a Roblox game needs to feel complete: the gameplay, the UI that drives it, the data behind it, and the performance that holds up when players join.

Solo development

I can take a feature from an idea to working game code, connect the pieces, debug what's failing, and optimize the parts that are slowing everything down. I prefer modular systems that are easy to extend over scripts that only work for one situation.

My scope covers scripting, gameplay, UI, saving, progression, and general systems work. I don't take on 3D modeling or advanced animation.

Performance

On one live project, I reduced server lag by about 40% by decoupling heartbeat loops and throttling remotes.

Player data

DataStore pipelines with session locking and retry handling help player progression survive failed requests and reconnects.

Gameplay systems

Reusable quest logic, dynamic weather, dialogue flows, physics interactions, and other mechanics designed to fit the game rather than one isolated script.
